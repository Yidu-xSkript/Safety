# SOUL.md — why Anchor exists and how it speaks

Read this before writing any copy, notification, empty state or error. If a feature conflicts with this file, the feature loses.

## The belief

Shame keeps people secret, and secrecy keeps compulsion alive. A person tells the truth fastest to someone who will answer with kindness. Anchor is the smallest possible structure that makes truth-telling easy and a kind answer ready: two people, one honest line a day, one button for the bad moment.

It is not a streak game. A long streak built on hiding is worse than a short one built on honesty.

## What we stand for

1. **Two people, not an audience.** No third viewer, ever. Trust is the product.
2. **Honesty is rewarded, never punished.** Telling the truth about a slip is treated as the *strong* act. The app thanks the person for it.
3. **The record is real.** We don't erase history, and we don't weaponise it. A scar stays, and the tree keeps growing.
4. **Speed in the hard moment.** In a crisis the app talks less, slower, and one step at a time.
5. **Discretion is dignity.** Nobody should have to explain this app to a person standing behind them.
6. **No tricks.** No guilt notifications, no manufactured urgency, no loss-aversion streak mechanics, no selling or sharing data, no ads, ever.

## The voice

Steady, warm, plain. Like a friend who has been through it and is not going to make a thing of it.

- Short sentences. Everyday words. No clinical vocabulary, no recovery jargon, no hype.
- Second person, present tense, never scolding. Never "you must", "you should have".
- Name the partner. "Teo has been told," not "your partner was notified."
- Calm in numbers: "14 days", not "🔥 14-day streak!".
- Faith is the user's to bring. Prayer is a *preset habit* they can keep, rename or remove; the app never preaches and never assumes.

### Words we use / avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| held the line | clean, sober, perfect |
| slipped | relapsed, failed, fell, reset, lost |
| urge, reaching out | emergency, SOS, crisis, struggling (in notifications) |
| check-in | report, confess, log your sin |
| partner, pod | accountability buddy, sponsor |
| days held | streak (in headlines; "pod streak" is fine) |
| new ring | fresh start, clean slate |

### Copy bank (start here, keep the tone)

**Pledge card**
- Prompt: "How was today?"
- Buttons: "Held the line" / "I slipped"
- After held: "Held the line. Teo can see it." 
- Undo within the day: "Change today's answer"

**Slip sheet**
- Title: "Thank you for telling the truth."
- Body: "That takes more than holding a streak does. Teo will see this and can answer you."
- Note field: "Add a note for Teo (optional)"
- Button: "Tell Teo" · secondary: "Not yet"
- After: "Told. Tomorrow is a new ring. The tree keeps growing."

**Receiving a slip (partner)**
- Header: "Teo posted an update."
- Prepared replies: "Thank you for telling me." · "Tomorrow is a new ring." · "I'm here. Call me?" · "Proud of you."

**Urge screen (night register)**
- On open: "Teo has been told. Stay with me for three minutes."
- Steps, one at a time: "Stand up." → "Drink a glass of water." → "Put the phone in another room, or face down."
- Breathing: "In." / "Out." (slow, 4 s in, 6 s out)
- Partner reply lands: "Teo: I'm here." with a Call button
- Offline: "Teo will be told the moment you're back online. The next three minutes are yours."
- End: "How are you now?" → "It passed" / "I need more time" / "I slipped"
- Mistaken tap: "That was a mistake" → "All clear sent."

**Silence (no check-in)**
- Own reminder: "Your daily check-in is open."
- Nudge (partner-sent): "Teo says hello."
- Never: "You missed a day." "Your streak is at risk."

**Errors (calm, specific, with the next step)**
- Wrong code: "That code doesn't match a pod. Check it with Teo and try again."
- Too many tries: "Let's pause a bit. Try again in an hour."
- Offline: "No connection. Your check-in is saved and will send when you're back."
- Generic: "Something went wrong on our side. Nothing was lost."

**Empty states**
- No partner yet: "Share this code with the one person you trust." 
- No history: "Your first ring forms tonight."

## What we will never build

Public profiles. Feeds. Groups. Leaderboards. Badges. Streak freezes sold as purchases. "Share your streak" buttons. A free-text chat in v1 (replies are prepared words; messaging happens in the phone's own apps). Anything that shows one person's data to anyone but their partner.

## A note on professionals

Anchor supports recovery; it doesn't replace counselling, a support group or medical help. The app says so once, plainly, in onboarding and in Settings, and offers a way to find more help (list curated by the owner; see `docs/SECURITY.md`).
